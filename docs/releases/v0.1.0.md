# MeetingMaster3 v0.1.0

The first release. MeetingMaster3 turns meeting audio, or an existing transcript, into a speaker-attributed
transcript and a structured Obsidian note on your own machine. Free, open source (Apache-2.0), single user, no
telemetry.

## Highlights
- **Audio to note, locally:** WhisperX transcription and pyannote speaker separation, named once by you, then
  notes written by a local Ollama model (or Bedrock / OpenRouter after your consent) into your vault.
- **24 editable templates**, each with a short and a detailed summary; the default, *Default Notes*, writes dense
  topic-by-topic notes.
- **Review workspace:** audio and transcript side by side, Preview or editor, per-section regeneration with
  diffs, template switching, speaker renaming, rename and re-date a meeting everywhere (backlinks included).
- **Search** by meaning and words, and **Ask-AI** for broad questions answered from your meetings with citations;
  answers are saved as linked notes.
- **Import** a transcript or an AI conversation (Markdown, VTT, SRT, or a Grok share link) without audio.
- **Docker Compose** for GPU and CPU, an **8 GB GPU** is enough (measured: a 60-minute meeting to a finished note
  in about 6-9 minutes).

Full list: [`CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md). Screenshots and a walk-through: [`README.md`](../../README.md) and
[`docs/user-guide/getting-started.md`](../user-guide/getting-started.md).

## Requirements
- Linux (developed and measured on Arch); macOS and Windows are not tested.
- **ffmpeg** and **ffprobe**; [uv](https://docs.astral.sh/uv/) for a native install, or Docker with the Compose
  plugin (and the NVIDIA Container Toolkit for the GPU image).
- An NVIDIA GPU with a CUDA 12.8 capable driver (570+) for fast transcription; CPU works, slowly.
- [Ollama](https://ollama.com) with a note-writing model (`qwen3:8b`) and `nomic-embed-text` for search, or a
  cloud backend with your own key.
- A free Hugging Face token to download the gated speaker-separation model once.
- About 15 GB of disk for the GPU image (4 GB CPU) and 2 GB of models; about 6 GB more for a native install.

## Known limitations
- No login: single user on `127.0.0.1`. Do not expose the port.
- English-first templates and cues; other languages transcribe with a warning.
- Measured on one 8 GB laptop GPU only; the other hardware rows are estimates.
- Tested in Chromium only (Firefox and Safari not tested).
- The `ml` extra carries upstream advisories that WhisperX's pins block; they concern loading untrusted model
  files, which the app does not do (ADR-0014).
- Some cloud models need extra parameters or settings (reasoning-only models, Mistral 7B/Mixtral on Bedrock).

More in the README and [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](../ROADMAP.md).
